Engineering note

This is an educational preliminary analysis, not a safe-to-build bridge design. The solver models ideal pin-jointed, linearly elastic 2D trusses and checks only simplified axial-stress and displacement thresholds. It excludes buckling, connections, lateral stability, foundations, material variability, dynamics, environmental actions, and design-code requirements. Never construct a bridge based on these results.
